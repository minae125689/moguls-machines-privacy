Moguls Machines

Moguls Machines is a personal tool used by its owner to manage their own YouTube channel: uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions and thumbnails, adding captions, and scheduling publishes.

It is a single-user tool for personal use. It is not a public service and it does not serve other users.
