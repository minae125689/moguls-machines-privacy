Privacy Policy — Moguls Machines

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What this tool is

Moguls Machines is a personal tool used by its owner to manage their own YouTube channel. It is a single-user tool for personal use only. It is not a public service and it does not serve other users.

Data the tool accesses

Through Google OAuth, and only with the owner's authorization, the tool accesses the owner's own YouTube account data needed to perform the actions the owner requests: uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and scheduling publishes. It also accesses basic account identity information required to sign in.

How data is used

Data is used only to carry out the actions the owner requests on their own channel. Authorization tokens are held only for the duration of the owner's session and are not stored on any server.

Data sharing

The tool does not sell, rent, or share the owner's data with any third party. Data is transmitted only to Google and YouTube services as required to perform the requested actions, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Data retention

The tool does not keep a separate copy of the owner's channel data. Video files and metadata exist on the owner's own devices and on YouTube, under the owner's control.
